The current AI manga/manhwa whiteout system is not precise enough.

CURRENT PROBLEM:
The AI sometimes removes or whitens areas outside the actual manga text, including parts of speech bubbles and surrounding artwork. In some cases it appears to treat the entire text region, bubble, or a large bounding box as the area to erase.

I do NOT want this behavior.

The required behavior is:

1. AI detects ONLY the actual visible text/glyphs.
2. ONLY the pixels belonging to the original text are removed.
3. Speech bubble borders, bubble backgrounds, artwork, characters, line art, shadows, and surrounding pixels must remain untouched.
4. The translated text must then be rendered precisely in the original text location, inside the correct speech bubble/panel.
5. The final result should look like a professionally translated manga page where the original text has been replaced, not where the speech bubble has been erased.
